About Us

Crafting premium health chews with uncompromised quality and scientific formulation.

Our History

Founded in Singapore, SmartChews began with a simple mission: to eliminate fillers and artificial preservatives from domestic pet functional treats. Through collaboration with prominent veterinary nutritionists, we developed a proprietary cold-pressing system that preserves critical minerals and enzyme bases, delivering authentic, systemic benefits to pets globally.

Our Employees

Our multi-disciplinary team features specialized lab researchers, quality control experts, and food scientists dedicated to continuous product evolution, safety optimization, and flavor fidelity.

Locations

Headquartered globally in Singapore with primary storage networks and manufacturing operations structured inside safe, ISO-certified cleanroom environments located nationwide.

Our Products

Every functional option we release relies strictly on all-natural compound structures, absolute trace origin transparency, and comprehensive testing vectors. We match structural chewing properties to real geometric bite forces, guaranteeing maximum surface friction control for clean teeth and healthy gums.
